## Intranet Design 2 by SharePoint Designs

🧩 **Design 2 Site Setup by SharePoint Designs**
Effortless SharePoint site creation starts here.
**Design 2 Site Setup** by **SharePoint Designs** is a ready-to-use application that empowers organizations to quickly deploy a beautifully designed, fully structured SharePoint intranet — based on our popular Design 2 layout.
Whether you're building a new departmental hub, intranet landing page, or team collaboration space, the built-in Setup Wizard walks you through every step — from organizational details and branding to template selection — so you can go from installation to a fully branded intranet in minutes.

---

## ✅ Key Features

- **Guided Setup Wizard** – A step-by-step wizard walks you through site configuration — organizational details, branding, and template selection — so you're up and running in minutes.
- **Always-Accessible from Any Page** – The Setup Wizard lives in the site header, so you can launch or revisit it from any page at any time.
- **Automatic Branding from Your Logo** – Upload your site logo and the wizard automatically generates a theme color palette and applies it across your site.
- **Custom Favicon** – Upload your own favicon directly from the Setup Wizard to give your intranet a branded presence in every browser tab.
- **Modern & Responsive Design** – Built to look great on desktop and mobile devices, with flexible layouts and customizable zones.
- **Consistent Branding** – Apply a standardized look and feel across all sites to reinforce your brand identity.
- **Flexible Data Sources** – Manage web part content directly in the settings panel using Collection Data, or connect to a SharePoint list — the choice is yours.
- **Productivity-Ready Components** – Includes Quick Links, Common Tools, News, Events Calendar, Holidays, and Top Navigation — ready to use out of the box.
- **No-Code Required** – Ideal for both IT admins and business users. Set up a modern SharePoint intranet without any technical overhead.
