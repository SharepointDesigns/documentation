# DocVault — Getting Started

## Signing in

DocVault runs inside your own SharePoint site — there is no separate login. Open the site your administrator gave you and you are already authenticated with your Microsoft 365 account. Everything you see respects your existing SharePoint permissions: if you cannot open a document in SharePoint, it will not appear for you in DocVault.

- - -

## Moving between screens

Every screen shares the same top navigation: **Home**, **My Workspace**, **Library**, **Search**, **Templates**, **Pipeline** and **Admin**. The current screen is underlined in your organisation's theme colour. Two buttons sit on the right:

- **Advanced search** — jumps to the Search & Discovery screen.
- **New document** — opens the Library with the upload dialog already open.

- - -

## Your trial

A new installation runs free for 15 days. A countdown banner appears across the top of every screen and turns red in the final three days. You can activate at any point during the trial without losing a day — select **Enter license key** in the banner. See [Installation & Setup](./installation.md) for full activation steps.
