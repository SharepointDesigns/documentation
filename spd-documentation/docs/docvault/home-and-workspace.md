### The welcome hero

The banner greets you by first name and shows your local time and date, refreshed every 30 seconds. Administrators can replace the background with a solid colour or an image (see [Templates & Administration](./templates-and-administration.md#branding)).

### Searching from Home

The white card overlapping the banner is the search entry point.

- Start typing — a type-ahead list of matching documents appears immediately, each showing its document number, department and status.
- Select the filter icon to the left to narrow by Department, Document type, Status or Classification. Selected values appear as counted chips and the badge on the icon shows how many filters are active.
- Press Enter, select the arrow button, or select **Show results** to open the full Search screen with your query and filters carried across.
- The **Copilot AI Search** toggle decides how the Search screen will answer: on for a grounded natural-language answer, off for a conventional full-text search.

### My Work

Three counters summarise your workload — **Pending approvals**, **Overdue reviews** and **Expiring soon** — and each opens My Workspace. Below them:

- **Needs your attention** — the three most urgent items, each tagged with why it is listed (rejected, expired, expiring soon, or awaiting your review) and an action label of Revise, Renew or Review.
- **Continue where you left off** — the four most recently modified documents.

### Department Libraries, Recently Updated, Top Contributors

The department cards show a live document count and open the Library already filtered to that department. Recently Updated shows the four newest documents as cards. Top Contributors ranks the five people who own the most documents.

## My Workspace

My Workspace is your personal queue. Four tabs sit inside the blue hero, each with a live count.

| Tab | What it contains | Action shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Needs attention | Anything pending approval, rejected, expired, or expiring within 30 days | Revise / Renew / Review |
| Assigned / Checked out to me | Documents waiting at either approval stage | Review |
| My drafts | Documents still in Draft | Edit |
| Recently viewed | The ten most recently modified documents | Open |

Selecting any row opens that document's detail page. Overdue items show the number of days overdue in red.
